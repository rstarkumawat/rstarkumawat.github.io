Privacy Policy

Arrow Nikalo · Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy explains what information the game "Arrow Nikalo" (the "app") uses, and why. We keep it short and collect as little as possible.

What the app stores on your device

Your game progress, settings and hints are saved on your own device. Uninstalling the app removes them.

What is sent to our game server

Only if you choose a username for the leaderboard, the app sends:

  • The username you picked. It is shown publicly on the leaderboard, so do not use your real name.
  • An app-specific device identifier. It is used to recognise your device so that your username stays yours and your progress can be restored after a reinstall. It is never shown to other players.
  • The number of levels you have completed, to place you on the leaderboard.

We do not ask for or store your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location. If you never choose a username, nothing is sent to our game server.

Advertising

The app shows ads using Google AdMob. Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID and similar data to show and measure ads, including personalised ads where the law and your choices allow. In regions where consent is required, the app asks for it before showing personalised ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads). See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Deleting your data

To have your username and leaderboard record removed from our server, email us at the address below and include the short Device ID shown in Settings. The steps are on the Delete your data page. Local data is removed by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.
