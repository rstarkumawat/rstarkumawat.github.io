Munimo

Apna hisab, apne haath — Udhar, kharcha, dukaan, kheti, sab ek app me

Munimo kya hai?

Munimo (package: com.merahisab.app) ek Android app hai jo kisaan, dukaandaar, mistri, majdoor aur har ghar ke liye banaya gaya hai. Isme aap apna lena-dena aur kharcha Hindi / Hinglish me aasaani se likh sakte hain — diary aur pen ki zaroorat nahi.

Kya-kya hai

  • Udhar khata — kisko diya, kisse liya, byaaj ke saath; WhatsApp / SMS se yaad dilayein
  • Roz ka hisab — ghar ka kharcha aur aamdani, mahine ki report
  • Kheti — har fasal ki lagat, upaj, bikri aur labh
  • Dukaan — kapde ki dukaan ka PDF bill, aluminium & glass ki napti, medical shop ka stock, batch, expiry aur party ka khata
  • BC / committee, hajri / majdoori aur tractor ka hisab

Aapka data surakshit

Saara data sirf aapke phone me rehta hai — hamara koi server nahi hai. Chahein to backup aapki apni Google Drive me hota hai. Poori jaankari ke liye padhein: Privacy Policy.
