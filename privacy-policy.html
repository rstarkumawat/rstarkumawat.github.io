Privacy Policy – Jaal Veer: Protector of Mahanagar

Developer: Nagesh Kumawat
Contact: rstarkumawat@gmail.com
Effective date: 07 November 2026

Short version: Jaal Veer does not need a login and never asks for your real name, phone number, email, contacts, photos or precise location. The game creates a random player ID so it can show the leaderboard. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which collects some device data as described below. You can delete all your data any time from Settings → Delete My Data inside the game.

Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card, UPI or bank details.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google may collect and share your device's Advertising ID, IP address (used to estimate general location), app interactions such as ad views and taps, and diagnostic information, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads). Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and can change their choice any time in Settings → Ad Privacy Choices inside the game. Buying “Remove Ads” turns off interstitial ads.

3. Service providers

  • Google Play (app distribution and in-app purchases)
  • Google AdMob (ads)
  • Our hosting and database providers (for example Render and MongoDB Atlas), which store the server data listed above on our behalf

We do not sell your personal data.

4. How long we keep data

  • Account, nickname, scores and mission data: until you delete your data, or 24 months after your last play.
  • Purchase records: kept for up to 8 years for refunds, fraud prevention and tax/legal reasons. After you delete your data they are no longer linked to your player ID.

5. Delete your data

In the game: Settings → Delete My Data → Delete. This erases your progress on the phone and your player profile and leaderboard entries on our server immediately.
Without the game (for example, if you already uninstalled it): follow the steps on our data deletion page. We complete email requests within 30 days.

6. Children

Jaal Veer is meant for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the game, contact us and we will delete the data.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the EU/UK GDPR), you can ask to access, correct or erase your data, withdraw consent, or complain to a data protection authority. Email rstarkumawat@gmail.com with your Player ID (shown in Settings). Our grievance contact for India is Nagesh Kumawat, reachable at the same email.

8. Security

Data between the game and our server is sent over HTTPS (encrypted). Server access is protected with signed tokens.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update this page and the effective date above.
