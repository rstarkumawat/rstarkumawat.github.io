Munimo – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026 · App: Munimo (package: com.merahisab.app) · Developer: Nagesh Kumawat, Behpur, Madhya Pradesh, India

Munimo is a simple record-keeping app for households, farmers, shopkeepers and workers in India. It helps you keep your own records such as udhar (credit) ledger, daily expenses, farm costs, shop bills, medical shop stock, BC/committee, labour attendance and tractor work. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is kept.

1. We do not run any server

All the records you enter are stored only on your own phone. The developer does not have any server or database, and we cannot see, collect, sell or share your records.

2. Information the app uses

  • Your records – entries, people's names and mobile numbers, bills, stock, etc. that you type. Stored on your phone only.
  • Profile – your name, mobile number and business name (optional). Stored on your phone and used on your bills and messages.
  • Google Sign-In (optional) – if you choose "Login with Google", the app receives your name, email address and profile photo from Google to fill your profile, and uses your Google account only to back up your data to your own Google Drive.
  • Contact picker (optional) – when you tap "Contact se chunein", your phone's own contact screen opens and the app receives only the one contact you choose (name and number). The app does not read your contact list and does not ask for contacts permission.
  • Photos (optional) – a shop logo you choose from your gallery, used only on your bills.

3. Backup

  • Google Drive backup (optional): your data is saved in a hidden app folder ("appDataFolder") of your own Google Drive. Only this app on your account can read it. The developer cannot access it.
  • Phone folder / backup file (optional): a backup file is saved on your phone or shared by you wherever you choose.

4. SMS, WhatsApp and sharing

When you choose to send a message, bill or report, the app opens your SMS app, WhatsApp or the share screen with the message ready. The message is sent only by you, from your own phone. The app does not send anything in the background. Shared messages and bills may include a small link to download Munimo; you can turn this off in Settings.

5. Notifications

The app shows reminders (for example BC kist or farm work) that you create. These are scheduled on your phone only.

6. Ads and payments

The current version has no ads and no in-app purchases. If ads or a paid subscription are added in future, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

7. Deleting your data

  • Delete any entry inside the app (deleted items stay in "Recently deleted" for 7 days, then are removed).
  • Settings → Account → "Logout + phone saaf" removes all data from the phone.
  • Uninstalling the app removes data from the phone. To remove the Google Drive backup, open Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Munimo → "Delete hidden app data".

8. Children

Munimo is meant for adults (18+) to keep business and household records. It is not directed at children.

9. Security

Your data stays on your device and in your own Google account. You can also turn on App Lock (fingerprint / phone PIN) in Settings.
