Privacy Policy – Shehar Rush

Last updated: 11-11-2026

This policy explains what information the game "Shehar Rush" (the "Game") uses. The Game is made by Nagesh Kumawat ("we").

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The Game has no accounts and no sign-in. Your progress (money, level, cars, settings, in-game photos) is saved only on your own device and is deleted when you uninstall the Game.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows ads using Google AdMob. Google may collect and use your device's Advertising ID, device and app information, and approximate location (derived from your IP address) to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), you are asked for consent first. Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings.

3. In-app purchases (Google Play)

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your card or bank details. Google's privacy policy applies to payments.

4. Notifications

If you allow it, the Game schedules reminder notifications (for example "your daily reward is ready") on your device. No information is sent to us. You can turn notifications off in your device settings at any time.

5. Voices

Character voices use your device's built-in text-to-speech engine. The Game does not record audio and does not use the microphone.

6. Sharing photos

When you choose to share an in-game photo, the Game opens your device's share menu. The photo is only sent to the app you pick (for example WhatsApp) and only when you choose to share it.

7. Children

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
