Delete your Jaal Veer account and data

App: Jaal Veer: Protector of Mahanagar · Developer: Nagesh Kumawat

Option 1: Inside the game (instant)

Open Jaal Veer → tap the ⚙ Settings button (top right) → DELETE MY DATA → DELETE.

Option 2: By email (if the game is uninstalled)

Send an email to rstarkumawat@gmail.com with the subject “Jaal Veer data deletion” and include your Player ID (shown at the bottom of the game's Settings screen) or the hero name you used on the leaderboard. We delete the data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your guest account (random device ID and player ID)
  • Your hero nickname and all leaderboard entries
  • Daily mission records
  • Game progress saved on your phone (coins, suits, upgrades) when you use Option 1

What is kept

Records of in-app purchases (product ID, Google Play purchase token and date) are kept for up to 8 years for refunds, fraud prevention and tax/legal reasons, but are no longer linked to your player ID. Items like “Remove Ads” can be restored through Google Play with Restore Purchases. Coins cannot be restored after deletion.

See also our Privacy Policy.
