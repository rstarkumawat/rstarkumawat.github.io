Delete your data

Arrow Nikalo (Android app, package com.nageshkumawat.arrow) · Developer: Nagesh Kumawat

This page explains how to ask for the data that the game "Arrow Nikalo" keeps about you on its server to be deleted.

How to request deletion

  1. Open Arrow Nikalo and go to Settings (the gear icon on the home screen).
  2. Note your Name and the short Device ID shown there.
  3. Send an email to rstarkumawat@gmail.com with the subject "Arrow Nikalo - delete my data" and include both.
Email a deletion request

We ask for the Device ID so that we delete the right record and nobody else can have your data removed. We will delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your player name (username)
  • The app-specific device identifier linked to it
  • Your level count and leaderboard position

After deletion your name is free for anyone to use, and you no longer appear on the leaderboard.

What is not kept

Nothing is retained after deletion. We do not store your real name, email address, phone number or location on our server. The email you send us is used only to handle your request.

Data on your phone

Game progress, settings and hints are stored only on your device. Remove them by uninstalling the app or by clearing its storage (Android Settings → Apps → Arrow Nikalo → Storage → Clear storage). If you open the game again after your server record has been deleted and still have a name saved on the device, the game may register that name again, so clear the storage or uninstall as well.

Advertising data

Ads are shown by Google AdMob. Data used for advertising is held by Google, not by us. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads, and manage your Google ad settings at myadcenter.google.com.
